I am an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at [The University of Texas at San Antonio (UTSA)](https://www.utsa.edu/), where I lead the **Trustworthy AI Lab**. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Software Engineering from Auburn University in 2023.

My research asks how we can make machine learning models reliable enough for high-stakes settings such as medical diagnosis. My group works on:

- **Trustworthy machine learning.** Finding and defending against the vulnerabilities of deep models to noise and adversarial perturbations. We also work on explainability, differential privacy, fairness, and **machine unlearning**, which means removing the influence of specific training data from a model. Our current focus is efficient unlearning, and unified frameworks that deliver robustness, fairness, privacy and interpretability together under practical training constraints.
- **Federated learning.** Training across many devices with different computing power, where data never leaves each device. We study graph applications in this setting, along with their adversarial vulnerabilities, fairness and unlearning.
- **Deep graph data mining.** Bringing deep learning and graph mining together to build graph analytics tools that are both accurate and scalable, for data such as social, biological and transportation networks.

We also apply these methods to healthcare, including explainable segmentation and rupture-risk classification of abdominal aortic aneurysms from CT scans.
